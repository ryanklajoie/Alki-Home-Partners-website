Grout Repair & Restoration

Contact for Pricing Priced on a case-by-case basis

Deteriorating grout is one of the most overlooked sources of water intrusion and structural damage in a home. Our grout repair and restoration service addresses cracked, missing, or discolored grout in tile surfaces throughout your home — protecting your investment and restoring a clean, finished appearance.

What's Included

  • ✓
    Full Assessment

    We evaluate the extent of grout failure across all affected surfaces — showers, floors, backsplashes, and exterior tile — and identify any underlying moisture issues.

  • ✓
    Grout Removal & Replacement

    Old, cracked, or compromised grout is carefully removed and replaced with color-matched material appropriate for the tile type and location.

  • ✓
    Sealing & Protection

    All repaired grout is sealed to prevent moisture infiltration, staining, and future deterioration.
